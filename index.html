About Matteo

👤 // profile_img

I’m Matteo, a mechanical engineering student with a focus on materials engineering and a growing interest in how design decisions play out in the real world. I enjoy building solutions that are not just technically sound, but practical, efficient, and considered.

My experience includes working as a building services consultant engineer, where I’ve applied engineering fundamentals to real systems in the built environment. I’m particularly interested in how materials, constraints, and performance come together — and where small decisions have large impacts.

Outside of engineering, I’m drawn to anything with a well-resolved mechanism- motorsport, mechanical watches, and getting hands-on working on motorbikes and cars. I’m especially interested in how things behave at their limits, and why.
